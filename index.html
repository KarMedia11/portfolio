ABOUT

Get to know me

I’m an Email Designer focused on responsive, brand-consistent email design, bringing a UI/UX foundation in Figma to email marketing.

My UI/UX work began when our team was asked to build a Job Request System from scratch for a university Maintenance Department,
so we conducted extensive research into their workflow, including order descriptions, job types, departments, and categories.
I translated our research findings and requirements into clear interface structures in Figma while working closely with the client, project manager, frontend and backend developer, then coordinated with the team to refine and present the system before the university break without putting the project deadline at risk.

You’ll get organized Figma files, clear communication, and responsive email designs delivered on schedule. I keep feedback visible, project updates straightforward, and design details consistent from the first draft to final handoff.
