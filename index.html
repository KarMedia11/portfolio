CASE STUDY • 01

How I Helped Riviera Rearrange The Message in 24 Hours

PROBLEM

The Riviera hero did not present the campaign message in a clear reading order, with the small banner placed above the main content and the CTA separated below it, making the key message harder to scan quickly. The body content also contained flavor mismatches, with the Peach Iced Tea section using Lemon Iced Tea copy and the Lemon Iced Tea section using Peach Iced Tea content, leaving the flavor information incomplete.

Solution

I reorganized the hero hierarchy so the Welcome to Riviera message leads into the delivery offer, code, and Shop Now CTA in a clearer reading order. I replaced the original image with a Mediterranean iced tea scene that supports the product message, keeping the color palette aligned with Riviera’s brand direction.

Result

The Riviera email redesign was completed within 24 hours. No claiming a measured campaign performance result.
